About me

I am a physical oceanographer interested in how multiscale dynamics regulate water-mass transformation, exchange, and ventilation in polar and subpolar oceans. I combine process-oriented numerical modeling with Lagrangian diagnostics, in situ and satellite observations, and analytical approaches.

My research spans Antarctic coastal polynyas, dense overflows across the Iceland–Faroe Ridge, and Greenland fjord dynamics. A recurring goal is to connect small-scale physical processes to their larger-scale consequences for ocean circulation, stratification, and climate-relevant tracers.

I received my Ph.D. from the MIT-WHOI Joint Program in 2024, an M.S. from Stanford University, and a B.Eng. from Zhejiang University.
